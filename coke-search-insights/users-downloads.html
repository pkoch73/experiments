What Content Gets Downloaded?

FIFA World Cup 2026 is the clear top tagged campaign with 4,924 downloads. But 47.5% of all downloads (13,974) have no proper campaign attribution — a significant metadata gap affecting ROI measurement.

Top 10 Campaigns by Downloads
▲
47.5% of downloads are unattributed — campaigns labeled "None (untagged)" or "unknown" account for 10,287 + 3,687 = 13,974 of 30,357 total downloads. Without campaign tagging, it's impossible to measure which creative programs drive the most asset usage. Recommend mandatory campaign tagging on asset upload.

Year-over-Year: Who's Growing, Who's Declining?

Overall downloads are down 11% YoY, but the story varies dramatically by region. INSWA (+167%), AFR (+107%), and ASP (+6%) are growing — while EU (−25%) and NA (−17%) are dragging the total down despite being the largest markets.
