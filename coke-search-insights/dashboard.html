Platform Overview

A high-level synthesis across all three reports reveals four systemic patterns shaping platform health.

Search-to-Download Gap
35.4% gap
87.5% of users search, only 52.1% download. 35.4% of users search but never find content worth downloading.

The Engagement Funnel

The platform loses users at two key stages. 12.5% of users never search. 47.9% of users never download. The 78.9% zero-result rate explains much of the drop-off.

Engagement Funnel — Users → Searchers → Downloaders
2,450 users search but don't download — they're being failed by the 78.9% zero-result rate. Better metadata would directly recover downloads.
1,812 users who do search never download — likely finding results but not relevant ones. Content gaps, not just search failures, are at play.
